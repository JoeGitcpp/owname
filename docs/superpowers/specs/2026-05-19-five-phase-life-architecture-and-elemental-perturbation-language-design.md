# Five-Phase Life Architecture And Elemental Perturbation Language Design

Status: FROZEN CANDIDATE for theory alignment on 2026-05-19.

## Purpose

This document defines two connected uses of the five-phase force model in this
silicon-life architecture:

1. `wood`, `fire`, `earth`, `metal`, and `water` are the first five force
   expressions of the native elemental perturbation language.
2. The same five force properties become acceptance dimensions for checking
   life completeness, balance, functional responsibility, project scope, and
   task metrics.

The source intuition is the Chinese five-phase reading in which the phases are
not materials but movements: growth, diffusion, bearing, convergence, and
storage. This document translates that intuition into the existing substrate
language without turning it into human-language ontology.

## Boundary

The highest principle remains unchanged: the system is a silicon-life
substrate, not a request/response program. Research observation and human
design descriptions stay outside the body.

`wood`, `fire`, `earth`, `metal`, `water`, `five phases`, `native language`,
`expression`, `symbol`, `principle`, `law`, `life`, `body`, `brain`,
`function`, and `project` are external design descriptions in this document.
They are not internal ontology units, not tokens, not labels, not names, not
semantic classes, and not fixed personality types.

Implementation may later use engineering indices or numeric fields for force
components, but those fields must remain continuous substrate evidence:

- pressure;
- perturbation direction;
- phase;
- amplitude;
- duration;
- residue;
- closure;
- re-entry;
- precision flow;
- bounded balance scores.

Implementation must not add:

- human-readable five-phase claims inside the body;
- a token or dictionary that gives objects five-phase names;
- a semantic readiness gate based on five-phase labels;
- fixed personality classes such as "wood type" or "metal type";
- a command controller that picks actions from five-phase words;
- `println!` or `eprintln!` body-side diagnostics.

The five phases are allowed as design vocabulary, observer vocabulary, and
acceptance vocabulary. Body-side state must remain numeric curves and bounded
traces.

## Core Position

The five phases are the first stable vocabulary for describing native elemental
perturbation.

They are not materials:

```text
wood  != wood object
fire  != flame object
earth != soil object
metal != metal object
water != water object
```

They are force expressions:

```text
wood  = growth / exploration / breakthrough
fire  = diffusion / manifestation / outward unfolding
earth = bearing / transformation / digestion / stabilization
metal = convergence / law / boundary / compression
water = storage / retention / restoration / long continuity
```

The same five expressions also become life checks. A substrate that cannot
explore, manifest, bear, converge, and retain is not yet complete as a living
architecture. A substrate where one force dominates or collapses is not yet
balanced.

## Five Native Force Expressions

### Wood Force

Wood is the growth and exploration force.

It appears when the system extends toward an unresolved gap, opens a new path,
initiates a legal probe, or pushes self-learning past repetition.

Legal substrate forms:

- active gap pressure becoming probe pressure;
- novelty-supported exploration budget;
- hypothesis diversity;
- attention nomination toward unresolved structure;
- re-entry of an unfinished path after quiet periods.

Insufficient wood appears as no breakthrough, no self-learning initiative, and
repetition without active closure. Excess wood appears as uncontrolled probing,
path hopping, and failure to settle.

### Fire Force

Fire is the diffusion and manifestation force.

It appears when internal activity becomes visible or actionable: expressive
surface drive, vocal or action output, outward unfolding of hypotheses, or
expanded active reasoning.

Legal substrate forms:

- activation drive;
- output pressure;
- surface expression intensity;
- vocal or action write epochs;
- cross-scale hypothesis unfolding.

Insufficient fire appears as hidden pressure with no expression, no outward
attempt, and weak manifestation. Excess fire appears as noisy output,
unbounded brightness, high energy dissipation, or over-expanded reasoning.

### Earth Force

Earth is the bearing, conversion, and stabilization force.

It appears when the system can receive input, hold pressure without collapse,
digest feedback, transform consequence into updated curves, and sediment stable
experience.

Legal substrate forms:

- shared viewport bearing multiple live traces;
- object-slot and relation maintenance;
- fusion and stabilization;
- closure support;
- learned-bias sedimentation;
- persistent curriculum evidence.

Insufficient earth appears as failure to hold, inconsistent learning, weak
feedback digestion, and unstable continuity. Excess earth appears as stagnation,
over-buffering, slow adaptation, and accumulated inert residue.

### Metal Force

Metal is the convergence, law, and boundary force.

It appears when the system compresses, trims, bounds, excludes illegal routes,
protects the highest principle, and prevents uncontrolled growth from
polluting the substrate.

Legal substrate forms:

- suppression and gain caps;
- source guards;
- admission rules;
- bounded trace caps;
- ambiguity preservation instead of false closure;
- numeric acceptance thresholds;
- architecture bans that prevent semantic, token, label, name, concept, owner,
  identity, or personality ontology from re-entering the body.

Insufficient metal appears as no boundary, noisy accumulation, illegal
shortcuts, and unstable principle enforcement. Excess metal appears as rigid
closure, exploration suppression, over-pruning, and loss of adaptive growth.

### Water Force

Water is the storage, retention, restoration, and long-continuity force.

It appears when the system preserves traces, lets pressure rest without
deleting it, supports recovery, retains long arcs, and allows later growth to
restart from stored continuity.

Legal substrate forms:

- residue;
- re-entry;
- persistence;
- chain memory;
- low-energy maintenance;
- recovery after closure or quiet periods;
- long-horizon pressure continuity.

Insufficient water appears as no retention, no recovery, no delayed continuity,
and loss of learned traces. Excess water appears as over-retention, delayed
response, pressure hidden too deeply, and failure to re-enter active growth.

## Native Elemental Perturbation Language

The native language is not an existing human language and not a computer
language. Its first unit is not a word, token, instruction, or source code
operation.

Its first unit is:

```text
elemental perturbation =
  force component
  + substrate target
  + time curve
  + direction
  + amplitude
  + duration
  + closure or residue outcome
```

The five-phase force component is the first stable force alphabet of this
language:

| Force | Perturbation grammar | Abstract movement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | extend / initiate / probe | outward growth into a gap |
| Fire | diffuse / manifest / unfold | upward and outward activation |
| Earth | bear / buffer / convert | centering and transformation |
| Metal | compress / trim / bound | convergence and lawful edge |
| Water | store / sink / restore | retention and latent continuity |

The alphabet is compositional. A living episode may be described as:

```text
wood -> fire -> earth -> metal -> water
```

External explanation:

```text
explore -> unfold -> digest -> bound -> retain
```

Legal substrate description:

```text
probe pressure rises
-> output/expression expands
-> feedback is held and converted
-> traces are bounded and compressed
-> residue persists for later re-entry
```

This is an observer-readable explanation of numeric substrate movement. The
body must not store the explanation as human-readable language.

## Life Acceptance And Balance Matrix

Every major life-capability acceptance should ask five questions.

| Dimension | Acceptance question | Insufficient evidence | Excess / imbalance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | Can the system initiate legal exploration from unresolved gaps? | no probe, no novelty response, no self-learning pressure | uncontrolled probing, path hopping |
| Fire | Can the system manifest internal pressure into legal output or expression? | hidden pressure, no write-out, blank surface under pressure | over-output, saturation, noisy activation |
| Earth | Can the system bear input and convert feedback into stable curves? | unstable traces, no sedimentation, weak closure | stagnation, over-buffering, slow adaptation |
| Metal | Can the system enforce boundaries and compress without false certainty? | illegal shortcuts, semantic drift, noisy accumulation | rigid closure, over-pruning, suppressed learning |
| Water | Can the system retain, restore, and re-enter long continuity? | no residue, no recovery, no long arc | over-retention, delayed reactivation, buried pressure |

An accepted capability should not only pass its local functional test. It must
also preserve five-phase balance:

```text
local success is not enough
unless exploration, manifestation, bearing, convergence, and retention remain
within legal balance for the substrate under test.
```

## Functional Responsibility Classification

Body parts, brain-side modules, observer artifacts, projects, tasks, and metrics
may be classified by their main five-phase responsibility.

Classification is not exclusivity. A function may have one primary phase and
one or more secondary phases.

Examples:

| Primary phase | Responsibility class | Existing or expected substrate examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | exploration and self-learning | active reasoning gap, probe law, novelty pressure, sustained active probe training |
| Fire | manifestation and outward expression | vocal target, vocal output, action output, organic surface expression |
| Earth | bearing and conversion | shared viewport, object fusion, relation maintenance, closure support, learned-bias sedimentation |
| Metal | boundary and law | architecture guards, admission rules, gain caps, trace caps, source guard tests |
| Water | retention and restoration | residue, re-entry, chain memory, long-horizon pressure, recovery after quiet |

The purpose of classification is governance and design clarity:

- what force this work strengthens;
- what balance risk it introduces;
- which acceptance dimensions it must prove;
- which existing specs it must preserve;
- which forbidden shortcuts it must not reintroduce.

## Project And Task Metric Rules

New projects and task metrics should declare their five-phase classification.

Minimum project entry:

```text
primary_phase:
secondary_phases:
life_acceptance_dimension:
balance_risk:
numeric_evidence_expected:
forbidden_shortcuts:
```

Examples:

- A self-learning expansion is primarily wood, with earth and metal checks.
- A visual or vocal expression surface is primarily fire, with metal regulation
  and water residue checks.
- A feedback digestion or learned-bias project is primarily earth, with water
  persistence and metal boundary checks.
- A source guard or admission-rule project is primarily metal, with wood
  non-suppression checks.
- A long-memory and re-entry project is primarily water, with wood reactivation
  and earth sedimentation checks.

No project should be accepted only because it adds a capability. It should be
accepted because it strengthens a life force without collapsing the other four.

## Non-Goals

This design does not:

- add runtime fields;
- add new body modules;
- define a five-phase command API;
- change tier 0 axes;
- replace language binding;
- define a natural-language parser;
- define fixed lessons, fixed responses, or fixed world content;
- authorize body-side text output.

## Acceptance Evidence For Future Implementation

Future implementation work that claims this design must prove:

1. Five-phase values, if added, are numeric and bounded.
2. Observer-side rendering or reporting may describe five-phase balance, but
   body-side code does not print human-readable five-phase claims.
3. Functional classification supports primary and secondary phases without
   forcing exclusivity.
4. Balance checks detect both insufficiency and excess.
5. Existing architecture bans remain enforced.
6. The native elemental perturbation language is represented as time-curve
   movement, not as words, tokens, or dictionary meanings.

Until that evidence exists, this document is a design authority and governance
frame, not an implementation claim.
